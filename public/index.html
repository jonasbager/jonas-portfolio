// Leadership

How I lead

Three things I've learned from building and managing cross-functional teams.

🔀

Align first, execute second

I get engineering, commercial, and leadership on the same page before we write a line of code. Misalignment at the start compounds into wasted sprints at the end.

🤝

Stakeholders as partners

I treat stakeholders as informed collaborators, not approval gates. Transparent roadmaps, honest trade-offs, and no surprises in the boardroom.

🌱

Unblock, don't micromanage

Small teams outperform big ones when they're trusted and unblocked. My job is to set clear context, remove friction, and let good people do great work.
